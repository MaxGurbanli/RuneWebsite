Update v1.12

On January 4th, 2022


This is a big economy-focused update bringing a new mining system.

Mine: A new fun way to earn coins
- Added !!mine command
-- You can start mining as soon as you acquire the new pickaxe item from the shop for 150rc
-- You can mine 3 times until your pickaxe needs to recharge for 1 hour, meaning it's best to use all 3 of your mine abilities at once to save time



- Added !!ores view and !!ores sell commands
-- Use these to view and sell your gathered ores from your mining trips



- New backpack item in the shop
-- By default you can hold up to 4 ores at once before you need to sell them to continue mining
-- You get 2 ores per mining trip, meaning the ore inventory will fill after in 2 trips
-- With a backpack, you can hold up to 12 ores at once



Emoji-picker: A new task added to our growing collection
Remember the emoji and select it from the list of available ones.
- Added emoji-picker mini-game to work and heist commands.
-- In the heist command this replaces the math task.

Trending: A dive into your favourite sub-reddits
See the latest trending posts on your favourite subreddits.
- Added !!trending [subreddit] command



Bug fixes
Not many bugs this time around. Phew...
- Fixed shields not being removed from the inventory after being used
- Various other minor performance improvements and bug fixes

That's all for now! A lot of this comes from your feedback - feel free to share your thoughts in our Discord server.
