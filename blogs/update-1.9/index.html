Update v1.9

On December 9th, 2021


This is another economy-focused update as a follow-up to v1.8, bringing even more exciting additions!

Shield: Protect yourself from grenades
- New shield item now in the shop.
Description: Protect yourself from grenades
Duration: 30 days after activation, or if broken by a grenade earlier
Cost: 150


The "Shield active" status is displayed when using the !!balance command, meaning you can check if someone has a shield active before throwing a grenade at them by using the balance command on them (!!balance @Member/ID)


The target is now notified in DMs whenever someone throws a grenade at them. This happens despite the outcome of the grenade.


Lots of price changes!
The following changes have been made to buy and sell prices of items:
- Grenade price reduced: 300 -> 200
- Hunting rifle price reduced: 200 -> 100
- Fishing pole price reduced: 200 -> 100
- Shovel price reduced: 200 -> 100
- The sell price of all items has been increased to their buy price

That's all for now! A lot of this comes from your feedback - feel free to share your thoughts in our Discord server.
