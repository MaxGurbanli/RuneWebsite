Update v4.x Thread

Last updated August 31, 2026.


This is an update thread for change notes of all v4.x updates. This blog is updated when a new update is released.

Update v4.0.1

Improvements

  • Revamped /inventory to organize items by category and prevent errors due to message size getting too large

Update v4.0 - Main Street

Businesses

  • /business upgrade takes a business from tier 1 to tier 5, revenue is 50 coins per tier
    • Upgrade costs: 500, 1,500, 4,000, 10,000 coins
  • Businesses now have condition, dropping 5% a day
    • Below 70% revenue drops by a quarter, below 30% it halves, at 0% the business stops
  • /business maintain repairs to 100% for 100 coins, 5 ores and 2 wood, or instantly with a :toolbox: Toolbox
    • Every business that already exists starts at 100% today
  • /business payroll sets an employee's cut of a shift they work, the rest goes to you (default 50%)
  • /business supplies stocks the consumable your business uses, 1 per shift, without them you won't receive tips
  • /business decorate puts up decor for better tips, 1 slot at tier 2 up to 4 at tier 5
  • /business ledger shows what a business has earned and spent
  • /business leave lets an employee quit
  • All business related spending now comes out of your bank account instead of your wallet

New mini-games

  • New /digsite command, 6h cooldown, needs a :shovel~1: Shovel
    • Uncover as many tiles as you dare, but hitting a cave-in will cost you the Surface bonus of 25 coins/tile.
    • Crafting a :wood: Support Beam will protect you from one cave in!
  • New /vault command, crack a 4 symbol code in 6 tries, 6h cooldown, needs a :laptop: Laptop
    • Pays 100 coins and a :key: Vault Code, which automatically finishes the hacking setup on a bank heist

New items

  • Supplies: :coffee: Coffee Beans, :chocolate_bar: Cocoa Pods, :hotdog: Sausage Links, :fuelpump: Jet Fuel, :carpentry_saw: Saw Blade, :firecracker: Dynamite, :receipt: Deposit Slips
  • Decor: :placard: Neon Sign, :potted_plant: Potted Fern, :radio: Jukebox, :bulb: Chandelier, :tropical_fish: Aquarium, :frame_photo: Wall Mural
  • Craftables: :toolbox: Toolbox, :wood: Support Beam
  • Buyable :lock: Safe, each one you own keeps 1,000 coins in your wallet out of reach of /rob

Changes

  • /rob now takes 25% of a wallet instead of 100%
  • Map Fragment drop rate 1/120 -> 1/30 (/dig, /fish)
  • A :map: Treasure Map now finds the Sunken Hoard (renamed from Gold) every time you fish with it, instead of a 2% roll
  • The Sunken Hoard now pays coins plus two items
  • Added Airport and Chocolate Factory tips to match other businesses
  • Standardized Mining Operation payout to match other businesses

Bug fixes

  • Business commands now respond faster
  • Fixed pets bringing an unusable Fishing Pole or Hunting Rifle, affected users have been repaired
  • Fixed /pet feed incorrectly displaying price as 10 coins while correctly charging 5
  • Fixed /business hire job offer buttons breaking after a bot restart
  • /business delete now asks for confirmation and notifies employees
  • Fixed the Rune Inc bank's records drifting out of step with account balances
