What makes Rune great?

Here are just a few features that define Rune.

Economy

Mine, fish, chop, dig and hunt your way to a fortune. Craft better tools, keep your coins safe in the bank, and when honest work gets boring - rob someone, hack the mainframe or start a heist. There are pets too. Feed them, wash them, don't let them die.

Businesses

Start a business and hire your friends as employees. Run an Airport, Cafe, Chocolate Factory, Hotdog Stand, Lumber Mill or Mining Operation and complete daily minigames to earn an income.

Moderation & Server Tools

Auto-deletion of scam links, auto-role for new members, a custom prefix, purge, slowmode and a full set of channel management tools for server managers and moderators.

Advanced Logging

Easily set up logging for member events, role events, channel events, message events and command usage, all in their own separate logging channels. Never lose track of what happens in your server!

Fun & Games

Play our recreation of the popular card game Blackjack, quiz yourself with an endless supply of speed Trivia, or enjoy the freshest dad jokes delivered straight to your doorstep.

Surveys

Build a survey for your server, pick which channel the responses go to, and lock it when you're done. Blacklist the trolls.
